01 / Energy

Rwanda Energy Progress: Tracking SDG7

PythonpandasWorld Bank APIscikit-learnPlotly / StreamlitGit
Original write-up → Code →

Rwanda has pushed electrification further and faster than almost any of its regional peers, moving from a single-digit connection rate at the turn of the century to a national grid-and-off-grid system reaching a majority of households today. This project traces that curve district by district, separating grid connections from off-grid solar, and models how the remaining gap — concentrated in the Western and Southern provinces — is likely to close under current investment plans. A forecasting layer projects time-to-universal-access per district, built on a pipeline that pulls directly from the World Bank's SDG7 tracking series so the numbers stay current without manual re-entry.

The rural-urban split is the real story here: urban electrification has been close to saturated for years, while rural connections have had to be built almost entirely from scratch, often leapfrogging straight to off-grid solar rather than waiting for grid extension. That divergence — and the pace at which it's narrowing — is the core of the analysis.

02 / Poverty

Predicting the Multidimensional Poverty Index from Nightlight & Geo Data

PythonGeopandasSatellite/Nightlight dataXGBoostMLflowAWS S3
Original write-up → Code →

Monetary poverty and multidimensional poverty tell overlapping but distinct stories — a household can be above the income poverty line and still be deprived in housing, cooking fuel, or health insurance. This project models sector-level MPI using VIIRS nightlight intensity as a proxy for economic activity, layered with NISR survey indicators and geospatial covariates, in an XGBoost pipeline with full MLflow experiment tracking.

Under Rwanda's latest household survey, cooking fuel and housing materials are the two heaviest contributors to multidimensional poverty nationally, ahead of drinking water and health insurance — and the rural-urban MPI gap is more than double (0.164 vs. 0.065). Mapping that unevenness at the province and district level is where the nightlight-based model earns its keep: satellite brightness picks up local economic shifts years before the next household survey round confirms them.

03 / Development Indicators

20 Years of Rwanda's Development, in One Dashboard

SQLdbtPower BITableauPostgreSQLAirflow
Original write-up → Code →

Twenty years of household surveys, census rounds, and sector reports scattered across PDFs and spreadsheets, consolidated into one warehouse: a dbt transformation layer over PostgreSQL, refreshed on an Airflow schedule, feeding parallel Power BI and Tableau front ends — the pairing most commonly requested together in analytics postings today. The dashboard tracks economic, demographic, education, and health indicators from 2000 through the latest EICV7 round (2023/24).

The headline trend is a poverty rate that has been cut by nearly a third in under a decade, even after NISR's 2023/24 survey moved to a stricter, more granular measurement methodology — meaning the improvement is real rather than a methodology artifact. The dashboard flags where that progress has been uneven: the Southern and Eastern provinces still trail the City of Kigali by a wide margin.
